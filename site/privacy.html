Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

This policy explains what the VYBE app ("VYBE", "we") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. VYBE is only for people aged 18 and over.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and Google account id. If you sign in with a phone number: that number.
  • Date of birth. The date you enter when you sign up. We use it to keep VYBE for people aged 18 and over and to show your age on your profile. We never show the date itself.
  • Identity check. A selfie and a photo of a government ID. A trained person on the VYBE team looks at them to confirm that you are a real person, that the selfie matches the ID, and that the date of birth is right. They are never shown to other members, and we do not write down or keep ID numbers.
  • Your location. Your device's location while you use the app, to show people and meeting spots near you. Your exact location is stored on our servers and is never shown to other members; they only see a rounded distance (for example "about 2 km away"). Companion locations are also blurred to a grid before any distance is worked out.
  • Profile. What you choose to add: first name, photos, a short bio, city, languages, work, interests, lifestyle and personality, voice intro, and your preferences (who you want to meet, plans, age range).
  • Activity. Chats and their messages, bookings, saved people, blocks and reports, ratings, and your credit balance and history.
  • Reels. Short videos you post, with their captions. Other verified members can watch them until you delete them.
  • Meetups. Whether you accepted a meetup, when you tapped "I'm here" at the venue, and your private answer to "How did it go?". The other person never sees that answer; if you say you felt unsafe, our safety team reviews it.
  • Safety Hub. The name and phone number of a trusted contact, if you add one. When you tap "Send my location", the app reads your location at that moment and puts a map link in a message that you send yourself; we do not track you. The SOS button opens your phone's dialler.
  • Notifications. A notification token for your phone, so we can tell you about messages, meetup requests and your verification. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play. We receive the product, order id and status needed to grant credits or a plan. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes: device model, operating system version and technical details of the error, through Firebase Crashlytics.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show you nearby people and places, let you chat and book meetups, and keep your account and credits.
  • To keep members safe: verify identity and age, enforce the rules, act on reports, and block messages that share phone numbers, emails or other contact details.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

What other members see

Your first name, age, photos, the profile details you add, your rating and number of meetups, and a rounded distance. Never your exact location, email, phone number, ID or selfie.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase: hosts our database, files and servers.
  • Google: sign-in, Google Play payments, and Firebase Crashlytics crash reports.
  • The people you meet: what is listed above under "What other members see", plus your messages and booking details with them.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

These providers may store data outside India. They only process it to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, selfie and ID images, location, preferences, saved people and credits. Bookings you made stay in the other person's history, showing only the first name they already saw. Purchase records may be kept where tax or payment rules require it.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and photos at any time in the app.
  • Turn off location in your phone's settings (the app cannot show nearby people without it).
  • Delete your account in the app, or ask us to: see Delete your account.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or for something to be corrected, by emailing us.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access rules on our database let each member reach only their own private data, and ID images are stored in a private area no other member can open.

Children

VYBE is not for anyone under 18. Accounts found to belong to someone under 18 are removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the app for important changes.
