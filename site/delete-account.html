Delete your VYBE account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open VYBE and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Confirm with Delete for good.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email rk7033688851@gmail.com from the email address of your VYBE account (or include the phone number you sign in with), with the subject "Delete my VYBE account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos, voice intro and profile details
  • Your selfie, ID images and date of birth
  • Your location, preferences, saved people and blocks
  • Your credit balance and plan

Open bookings are cancelled and your chats are closed for the other person.

What is kept

  • Past bookings stay in the other person's history, showing only the first name they already saw.
  • Purchase records, where tax or payment rules require them.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Plus or Premium plan in Google Play. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions so you are not charged again.
