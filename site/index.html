Meet new people, safely.

VYBE is an app for finding friendly company nearby: someone to grab a coffee with, catch a movie, take a walk or go to an event. Every member verifies who they are, and every meetup happens at a public place.

Verified people

Members confirm their identity with a selfie and an ID check before they can meet anyone.

Public places only

Meetups are booked at cafes, restaurants, parks, cinemas and malls. Never at homes or hotels.

Your location stays private

Others only see roughly how far away you are, never where you are.

What VYBE is not

VYBE is for respectful, friendly company. Sexual services, solicitation of any kind, meeting in private places and taking conversations off the app are not allowed and lead to removal.
